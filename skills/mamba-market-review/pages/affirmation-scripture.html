      House rules

      The first three principles from How to Win Friends and Influence People: how I handle people, before anything else.

      1

      Don’t criticize, condemn, or complain.

      2

      Give honest and sincere appreciation.

      3

      Arouse in the other person an eager want.

      Dale Carnegie, How to Win Friends and Influence People (1936), Part One: Fundamental Techniques in Handling People.

      Liner notes
