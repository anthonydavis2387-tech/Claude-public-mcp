Food for thought · before you trade the index futures
“What’s good for the US currency is bad for the indices, and vice versa.”Your rule of thumb, from reading the news and Forex Factory

Said a little tighter: “A strong dollar is a headwind for stocks; a weak dollar is a tailwind.” It is the same idea behind the trading-desk line “good news is bad news”: hot US data lifts the dollar and yields because the Fed may stay tight, and the indices sell off on what looks like good news.

Why it usually works. A stronger dollar tightens financial conditions and shrinks the overseas earnings of US multinationals when they are converted back to dollars. Dollar strength often comes from higher yields or a hawkish Fed, which also pressure stock valuations.
When it breaks. In a strong-US-growth stretch the dollar and stocks can rise together, and in a US-specific scare both can fall together. Treat it as a lean, not a law: confirm with the 10-year yield and the news driving the move.
