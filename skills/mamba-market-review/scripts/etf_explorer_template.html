Before you invest, review three things

Every fund page below scores these for you under “Investing check”.

  1. 1
    HoldingsWhat you actually own. How many names, and how much sits in the top 10? A fund with 60% in ten stocks is a concentrated bet, whatever its name says.
  2. 2
    FeesThe expense ratio comes out every year, in good years and bad. 0.03–0.20% is typical for broad index funds; above 0.50% the fund needs a clear reason to cost more.
  3. 3
    LiquidityHow much trades each day. Heavily traded funds have tight bid–ask spreads; thin ones can cost you on the way in and out, so use limit orders.
